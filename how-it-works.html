◆ SAMADHAN

Report → Understand → Match → Assign → Track → Verify → Measure

Samadhan is designed to make public problem resolution more traceable, measurable, transparent and accountable. Every material action leaves an audit event.

01 REPORT

A citizen records the issue, place, urgency and affected people.

02 UNDERSTAND

Deterministic local analysis identifies domain, priority and needed expertise.

03 ASSIGN

Government matches a responsible demo organization and an SLA deadline.

04 VERIFY

Progress and evidence remain visible; only citizen verification closes a case.
