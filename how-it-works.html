A clear path from need to action

Good ideas need a place to travel.

Aerosols turns a local problem into a shared project, connecting lived experience with the people and resources needed to make change stick.

The Common Ground journey

Every step is visible to the people who submit, study, fund, build, test, and benefit from a solution.

01 / LISTEN

Bring the real issue

Citizens, communities, local bodies, and departments submit a challenge with evidence, location, and context.

02 / MAKE SENSE

Find the signal

AI-assisted triage categorizes, prioritizes, detects duplicates, and recommends the right expertise.

03 / BUILD

Form the team

Universities bring students, faculty mentors, research capability, and multidisciplinary thinking.

04 / ACCELERATE

Partner for reality

Industry, startups, MSMEs, and CSR organizations bring mentorship, funding, pilots, and technology.

05 / PROVE

Measure the outcome

Milestones, approvals, testing evidence, adoption, and community validation remain accountable.
